About · Stack

A small toolbox, used well.

I’m a web developer based in Indonesia, currently in the middle of shipping four public repos in one month and getting properly stuck into the part of the job that isn’t coding — naming things, planning migrations, deciding what not to build.

My stack is PHP — Laravel for projects where the package ecosystem pays for itself, CodeIgniter 4 when I want every line of the request to be readable. I’m exploring AI-assisted workflows this year (yes, this site is scaffolded by one), with the goal of staying curious without losing the craft.

Outside the keyboard: coffee, late nights reading changelogs, and a stubborn belief that gamification can fix almost any UI.
